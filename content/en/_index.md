# Welcome!

We are an educational hub focusing on mathematics and physics, with a team
of young and enthusiastic professionals who strive to do more than teaching.

## Why Study With Us?
Our firm conviction is that mathematics means much more than formulas and
theorems which students memorize, computations that span pages and pages
or exercises which follow boring and predictable patterns, but which you
have to solve to prepare for your exams.

Scientific and mathematical education has a distinct role,
more so during the present times, when artificial intelligence seems to
be capable of replacing you in anything.

School sometimes fails to deliver on its promises
or when it does deliver, it's not more than what you need for
good grades, neglecting actual *knowledge and understanding*.

Studying with us promises a friendlier and more diverse approach that most
schools or private tutors offer. Our teachers are experienced both in
academical excellence, and in teaching, so we all know that it is
equally important to know a theorem, but also to be able to *explain* it.

> Extremism, disinformation, misinformation, conspiracies
> and other truth- or information-related problems that are on the rise
> in many countries, including our own, can and must be countered with
> careful and good quality education.

## Modern Methods and Tools
Our teaching resources always contain notes and remarks on the history and
philosophy of science, applications of mathematics in natural science
or technology. Technically, they are written and typeset carefully
such that they are not just another page in your manual.

Education must first and foremost be accessible, so all our lessons,
courses, and workshops involve *free and open source* tools, such that
students or teachers who partake are not forced into buying books,
subscriptions, or other digital or physical items.
