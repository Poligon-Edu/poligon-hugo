# Educational Resources
Here you will find resources we recommend for learning mathematics,
with applications in natural science, historical development, philosophical
interpretations, and more.

Some of them will be written by our team, while others are authored by specialists
we appreciate and recommend: researchers, professors, journalists, writers,
or professionals of varying expertise.

We always strive to make all resources here available for free, because
unrestricted access to education is one of our fundamental goals.

Use the sidebar navigation to read more.
