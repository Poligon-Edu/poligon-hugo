# Adrian Manea, PhD.

Hey there!

I'm Adrian, founder and manager of Poligon Educational.

I have a PhD. in Mathematics since 2016 and have worked until 2026 as
a teaching assistant at the Faculty of Mathematics and Computer Science
of the University of Bucharest, and the Faculty of Applied Sciences
of the Politehnica University of Bucharest.

Poligon Educational started in 2023, but it was just a simple and natural
continuation of my passion for scientific and mathematical education.
In fact, I'm at my happiest when I share what I know or found out.
It's one of the reasons I became a teacher.

Much of the initial spark is due to some excellent professors I've
met during my academic education. I am grateful for having worked with
and learned from leading experts such as Sorin Dăscălescu, Dragoș Ștefan,
Victor Alexandru, Victor Vuletescu, and Paltin Ionescu. They all showed me
that everything becomes clearer and easier to understand, even the most
abstract constructions, when historical context is provided, or when
philosophical interpretations are discussed, and not least when
you keep in mind that researchers were chiefly *very curious people*.

What followed naturally was my collaboration with the Faculty of Philosophy
at the University of Bucharest. I attented courses of early modern philosophy
taught by Dana Jalobeanu, then co-taught myself, with Sorin Costreie and
Alexandru Dragomir. We teamed up for an undergraduate course in the Philosophy
of Mathematics and its Ontology. The discussions and debates that emerged
with Philosophy students were some of the most entertaining and thought-provoking
I ever had, totally different from what I was used to at the Faculty of
Mathematics or at Politehnica.

So I started Poligon — *many-sided science and geekery*. In January 2024,
my first journalistic article appeared, in a local magazine, a cultural
staple for decades, Dilema Veche. It was followed by increasingly more
articles, columns, book reviews, and editorials in local magazines
such as Dilema, Școala9, Mindcraft Stories, and Vocativ.

Poligon grew naturally and I am very happy to have joined forces with
Maria, Răzvan, and Joshua. Together, we strive to improve mathematical
and scientific education for curious minds of all ages.

You can find out more about me and my activity on my
[personal website](https://adrianmanea.xyz/en/).
